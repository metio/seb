[Calendar sync](/calendar-sync/) accesses the calendars and events of my own Google account and keeps them on my own computers. No data is shared with anyone, and no other person's account is ever accessed.

## Data accessed

With the scope `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar`, the client reads and writes the calendars and events of the Google account that signed in, which is my own. It reads no other Google data.

## Use and storage

Events are copied into a local calendar store on my computers, where my calendar programs read them and from where changes are written back to Google Calendar. The OAuth tokens are kept in an encrypted secret store on the same computers. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's, and no analytics or tracking of any kind is involved.

## Sharing

Calendar data is never sold, shared with or transferred to any third party, and is never used for advertising or to train any model.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Revocation and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time under [Third-party apps & services](https://myaccount.google.com/connections) of the Google account. Deleting the local calendar store removes every copy the client made.
